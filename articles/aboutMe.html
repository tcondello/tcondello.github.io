The story

The serious version is simple. I work on AI infrastructure and spend a lot of time helping teams build production-grade systems. The more interesting version is that I never really stopped treating the internet like a workshop.

Origin

I’m drawn to systems that look clean from a distance and get stranger as you inspect them up close. That includes search stacks, model behavior, OCR pipelines, infrastructure tradeoffs, and all the weird engineering decisions hiding under a polished product surface.

This site exists because social posts are too small for the full thread. A good idea usually comes with code, diagrams, false starts, half-finished notes, and a few questions that refuse to sit still. I wanted a place where all of that could live together.

Why this format

HTML gives me control over the writing environment itself. Not just the words, but the pace, the motion, the layout, and the little rewards for people who explore. I like pages that feel built, not poured into a template.

So this blog leans into hidden states, terminal prompts, source-level clues, and the feeling that something extra is waiting if you look for it. Old internet spirit. Better tools. Higher standards.

What I work on

A lot of my work sits between research and deployment. I care about what survives contact with real users, real data, and real cost constraints.
